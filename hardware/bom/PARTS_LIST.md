## Before you order

1. Buy the display module first and check its pin order and outline against J4.
2. Check every **VERIFY** LCSC number and its stock.
3. Check the battery lead polarity (pin 1 = BAT+) before plugging it in: a reversed pack destroys the charger.
4. Order the OPTIGA Trust M (U2) from Mouser or Digi-Key: SLS32AIA010MKUSON10XTMB2 (or ...XTMA2, or the -40..105 C SLS32AIA010ML...: same V3 chip and package). Buying it yourself from an authorised distributor also means you know where the chip your PIN protection depends on came from. Fitting it: 3 x 3 mm, 0.5 mm pitch with a pad underneath (floating, for mechanical support): use solder paste and hot air or a hot plate (hardware/README.md section 8).
5. Check JLCPCB's assembly preview (part rotations) before paying.
